### The Three Classic Problems of Ancient Greek Mathematics
1. Given a circle, construct a square with the same area (Squaring the circle)
2. Given an arbitrary angle, trisect it (Angle trisection)
3. Given a cube, construct a cube with twice the volume (Doubling the cube)

### Archimedean Spiral
On a plane, let a ray OB rotate around a fixed point O at a uniform speed. Assuming the initial position is OA, when point P starts from O simultaneously as the ray begins moving from OA, and moves along OB at a constant speed, the curve drawn by point P is an Archimedean spiral.
