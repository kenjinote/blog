# Splatoon 2
Splatoon 2 is probably the game I've played the longest in my life.
I've barely played the story mode, but the 4v4 online matchmaking is very well done. Being able to play against people around my skill level gives it a captivating appeal that never gets old. Also, it is fun to read the movement patterns of both opponents and teammates and think of strategies to win.

# Weapons
The weapons I use most often, in order, are the "Foil Squeezer", "Splattershot Jr.", "Splat Roller", and "Foil Flingza Roller".
The "Splattershot Jr." is a beginner-friendly weapon, and I used it a lot in the early stages.

The turf inked points for each are as follows:

| Weapon | Turf Inked |

# Regarding Level
I've been going back and forth between A+ and S+, and I've been stuck unable to climb to the upper tiers of S+ for a while now. However, rather than trying to climb to the top, I feel like I'm playing just to enjoy the matches.
(If I get too high up, the opponents get stronger and the tension rises, making it less enjoyable.)

# Play Time
My playtime has exceeded 2,500 hours.

# Future Goals
My goal is to maintain my game level and have fun playing, but I would like to max out my money and gear.
Since Splatoon 3 came out, matchmaking has been taking longer and sometimes fails entirely, so I want to keep playing while hoping the player base doesn't decrease any further.
