# Purchased a mac mini (2018)

I bought an older used mac mini (2018) on Mercari. The specs are below.

- CPU: 3.2GHz 6-Core Intel Core i7
- Memory: 32GB
- Storage: 1TB SSD

The price was 77,000 JPY

# Reason for purchase

- I use a mac mini with almost the same specs at work for smartphone app development, but I wanted to set up a development environment at home as well (especially for running Xcode).
- I have a macbook pro, but it's 9 years old and runs very slowly, so I hardly use it.
- I wanted to develop iOS apps personally.

# Immediately installed Xcode and IntelliJ IDEA

The Xcode version is 14.3 (14E222b)
