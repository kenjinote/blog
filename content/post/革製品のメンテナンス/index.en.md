Recently, I have become interested in leather products and have been purchasing various items. Along with this, I've developed an interest in the maintenance of leather products and have gathered some related items, which I would like to introduce.

# Recently Purchased Leather Product Maintenance Items

## M.MOWBRAY DELICATE CREAM
![image1.JPEG](image1.JPEG)

Price: Around 1,320 yen

This cream provides nourishment, moisture, and flexibility.
It doesn't add gloss, but it is suitable for leather that requires a lot of moisture and hydration.
It doesn't have an unpleasant smell, but it does have a slight scent.
Since it's colorless, I think it's highly versatile. Because it's a light and smooth cream, it might be good to use a bit more of it compared to other creams.

## Collonil 1909 Supreme Creme de Luxe
![image3.JPEG](image3.JPEG)

Price: Around 1,777 yen

This is a famous, standard, and all-purpose cream that is less likely to cause stains. It is a moisturizing cream that restores the color of the leather.
It gives a slight gloss. It is white and seems to also offer water-repellent effects.

## M.MOWBRAY SHOE CREAM (Black)
![image2.JPEG](image2.JPEG)

Price: Around 1,000 yen

A cream for shoes. It contains wax, oils, and organic solvents.
It restores the color of the leather and adds gloss. Since it's black, apply it to black leather.
I use it for my suit shoes.

## M.MOWBRAY STAIN REMOVER

Price: Around 700 yen

![image4.JPEG](image4.JPEG)

This came with a brush I bought.
A water-based cleaner for shoes. It removes dirt from leather.
Soak a cloth generously with it and gently rub the leather to remove the dirt.
