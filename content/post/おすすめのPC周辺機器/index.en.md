# Recommended & Desired PC Peripherals for an Active Engineer

The ownership and usage notes in this article reflect the original publication date, February 28, 2023. I could not confirm where the Amazon short links currently lead or whether those items are in stock. Before buying, check the exact model, layout, price, and region; consult the manufacturers for current specifications. Personal comfort varies by user and task.

## Keyboard

When it comes to keyboards, I highly recommend those with a durable capacitive non-contact (electrostatic) switch.

At the time of the original post, I had owned a [REALFORCE](https://amzn.to/3IzOOFO) for over 10 years and was still using it.
I did not record the exact model of the keyboard I own. REALFORCE has several current series, so check the layout, quiet-switch option, and key weight before choosing. [Official REALFORCE product lineup](https://www.realforce.co.jp/en/products/)

At the time, I used the quiet version with an English layout.

The model I was interested in when this post was first published was MX Keys. As of this review, Logitech lists MX Keys S as a current model, but I have not tried it. See the [official Logitech page](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/shop/p/mx-keys-s?crid=27) for its specifications. [original Amazon Japan link](https://amzn.to/3Z5skmR).

## Trackball

A trackball lets you move the pointer without moving the whole device across the desk. I prefer that feel, but comfort depends on the person and the task.

The model I used at the time of the original post was MX Ergo. Logitech’s updated MX Ergo S is described on its [official product page](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/shop/p/mx-ergo-s-wireless-trackball-mouse.910-007249); I have not tried the MX Ergo S. [original Amazon Japan link](https://amzn.to/3IYOwtf).

Another option I was considering is ELECOM DEFT PRO (M-DPT1MRXBK), an index-finger-operated trackball. The manufacturer lists a ball diameter of about 44 mm. [Official ELECOM product page](https://www.elecom.co.jp/products/M-DPT1MRXBK.html) [original Amazon Japan link](https://amzn.to/3Zu5tB8).

## Display

A 4K or ultrawide monitor can help when you want more workspace. The right resolution and size depend on the task, viewing distance, operating-system scaling, and your computer’s ports, so check compatibility before buying.

At the time of the original post, I used a [DELL 4K display](https://amzn.to/3y0ViIW).

At the time, I was also considering a wide display from [EIZO](https://amzn.to/3kz7BZH) or [DELL](https://amzn.to/3xZleo1).

Current product ranges: [Dell 4K monitors](https://www.dell.com/en-us/shop/monitors/ar/all-monitor/appref%3D4k-uhd-3840-x-2160-screen-resolution) and [EIZO FlexScan monitors](https://www.eizo.com/products/flexscan/flexscan_ev.html/). The exact models behind the Amazon links in this article could not be confirmed.
