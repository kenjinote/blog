# Recommended & Desired PC Peripherals for an Active Engineer

## Keyboard

When it comes to keyboards, I highly recommend those with a durable capacitive non-contact (electrostatic) switch.
I have owned a [RealForce](https://amzn.to/3IzOOFO) for over 10 years, and it is still working perfectly today.
The model I use is the English layout, silent version.

However, recently I've also been interested in the [Logitech MX Keys](https://amzn.to/3Z5skmR) keyboard.

## Trackball

For pointing devices, I recommend a trackball over a mouse, as it reduces fatigue.

I currently use the [Logitech MX Ergo](https://amzn.to/3IYOwtf). This is an expensive device costing over 10,000 yen, but I think it is well worth the price.

I am also interested in the [DEFT PRO (M-DPT1MRXBK)](https://amzn.to/3Zu5tB8), which has a large ball and is operated with the index and middle fingers.

## Display

Any display will do, but I recommend a high-resolution 4K display.

Currently, I use a [DELL 4K display](https://amzn.to/3y0ViIW).

Right now, I would love to have a wide display like an [EIZO display](https://amzn.to/3kz7BZH) or a [DELL display](https://amzn.to/3xZleo1).
