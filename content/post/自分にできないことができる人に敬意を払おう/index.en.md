# Let's show respect

Let us respect people who can do things that we cannot.

In society, there are surprisingly many people who rank jobs or positions and are unable to show respect to others.

Let's pay respect to anyone who accomplishes a job we cannot, regardless of what that job may be.

This is something I want to keep in mind.
