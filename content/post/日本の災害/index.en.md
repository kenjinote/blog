# 2024 Noto Peninsula earthquake: overview and damage snapshot

At 16:10 Japan Standard Time on January 1, 2024, a magnitude 7.6 earthquake struck the Noto region of Ishikawa Prefecture, reaching a maximum seismic intensity of 7 on Japan's scale. The Japan Meteorological Agency named this event and the related sequence of seismic activity since December 2020 the 2024 Noto Peninsula Earthquake. See the [JMA information page](https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/menu/20240101_noto_jishin.html) for details.

## Damage snapshot

In report 123, dated March 31, 2026, Japan's Fire and Disaster Management Agency listed 720 deaths, including 492 disaster-related deaths, 2 people missing, and damage to 165,813 residential buildings. These are preliminary figures in that report and may be revised. See the [agency report](https://www.fdma.go.jp/disaster/info/items/b055402cefac7721623c30034b25489118dbec38.pdf) for definitions and the full breakdown.
