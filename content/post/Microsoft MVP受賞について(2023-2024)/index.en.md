# About Receiving the Microsoft MVP Award (2023-2024)

I have received the Microsoft MVP (2023-2024) award.
This is the 7th consecutive year I have received this award since 2017.

The category I was awarded in is **Developer Technologies**.

Looking back at my past activities, I have been doing the following:

- Publishing code on [GitHub](https://github.com/kenjinote)
- Disseminating technical information on my blog and [SNS (Twitter, etc.)](https://twitter.com/kenjinote)
- Posting on the [MSDN Forums](https://social.msdn.microsoft.com/Forums/ja-JP/home)
- Providing feedback on Microsoft products

I hope to continue writing better articles and sharing information.

Thank you very much.
