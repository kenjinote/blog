Here are my 3 recommended movies featuring mathematician protagonists.

# A Beautiful Mind
![](./images/beautiful_mind.webp)  
A film depicting the life of a real-life American genius mathematician. A surprising twist at the end.
Russell Crowe's performance in the lead role is excellent.

# Enigma
![](./images/enigma.webp)  
> A 2001 film adaptation of Robert Harris's novel "Enigma".
> — Wikipedia

A story about decrypting the codes generated by the German Enigma cipher machine.

# The Imitation Game
![](./images/imitation_game.webp) 

Another story about cryptanalysis dealing with the Enigma. I love the atmosphere of that era.

# The Man Who Knew Infinity
![img.png](img.webp)

A film depicting the interaction between Cambridge University mathematician G.H. Hardy and Indian mathematician Srinivasa Ramanujan. You can feel the beauty of mathematics.

# The Theory of Everything
![img_1.png](img_1.webp)

A film depicting the life of Dr. Stephen Hawking.
