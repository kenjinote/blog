# 6 Films About Mathematics, Codebreaking, and Physics

Here are six films and documentaries about mathematicians, codebreakers, and physicists. Dramas based on real people or events may change scenes and timelines for storytelling, so enjoy them as films rather than as historical sources.

## Dramas about mathematicians and codebreakers

### A Beautiful Mind

![](./images/beautiful_mind.webp)

This biographical drama starring Russell Crowe is inspired by the life of mathematician John Nash. It includes dramatic changes and is not a literal account of his life.

### Enigma

![](./images/enigma.webp)

This 2001 fiction film is based on Robert Harris’s novel. It is a thriller set against codebreaking at Bletchley Park during World War II, not a biographical film about Nash or Turing.

### The Imitation Game

![](./images/imitation_game.webp)

This drama portrays mathematician, logician, and codebreaker Alan Turing and wartime codebreaking. It is based on a real person, but includes cinematic dramatization.

### The Man Who Knew Infinity

![](./images/img.webp)

The film tells the story of Indian mathematician Srinivasa Ramanujan and Cambridge mathematician G. H. Hardy. See our [profile of Ramanujan](/en/p/ramanujan/) as well. It is a drama based on real events.

## Scientists and research in action

### The Theory of Everything

This biographical drama follows theoretical physicist Stephen Hawking and Jane Hawking’s relationship. It is based on Jane’s memoir.

### Particle Fever

This documentary follows physicists working with the Large Hadron Collider (LHC), including research surrounding the Higgs boson. It offers a different perspective from narrative films about mathematicians.
