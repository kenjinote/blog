# Immediate Tasks
1. Clarification of Goals and Vision  
   Purpose: Why create the software?
   Example: Solve a problem, provide new value, target a specific niche market, etc.
   Vision: What kind of future do you want to realize through the software?
   Example: Improve business efficiency, make daily life more comfortable, improve the quality of education, etc.
2. Market Research and Needs Analysis  
   Target User: Who will use the software? Specify age group, occupation, interests, etc.
   Competitor Analysis: Are there competitors in the same field? Analyze their strengths and weaknesses.
   Identifying Needs: Understand the unresolved problems and demands of the target users.
3. Software Design and Features  
   Basic Features: List essential features. Example: User authentication, data storage, analysis functions, etc.
   Differentiation Points: Clarify unique points compared to other companies' products. Example: Simple UI, utilization of AI, high customizability.
   Technical Specifications: Programming languages, frameworks, databases to be used, etc.
4. Development Plan and Resource Allocation  
   Development Schedule: Set a timeline for each stage of the project. Example: Prototype development, testing, official release.
   Team Composition: Required personnel and skill sets (programmers, designers, marketing staff, etc.).
   Budget: Estimate development costs, marketing costs, and operating costs.
5. Marketing and Revenue Model  
   Promotion Methods: Utilize SNS, websites, advertisements, etc. to improve awareness.
   Monetization Strategy: Consider options such as a free version + paid version, monthly subscription, advertising revenue, etc.
   User Acquisition Strategy: Providing free trials, promoting word of mouth, forming user communities, etc.
6. Operations and Scaling  
   Post-release Operations: Collecting feedback from users, fixing bugs, adding features.
   Growth Strategy: Plan how to increase the number of users, expand to other countries, and develop new features.
   Risk Management: Prepare countermeasures for legal issues and technical troubles.
