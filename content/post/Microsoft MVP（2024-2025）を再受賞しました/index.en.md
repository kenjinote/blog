# Awarded Microsoft MVP (2024-2025) Again

![img\_1.png](img_1.png)

Hello, this is kenji.
I'm pleased to announce that I have been awarded the **Microsoft MVP (Most Valuable Professional)** again this year. This is my 8th time receiving the award.

Since I have the opportunity, I'd like to write a little bit about what the MVP is.

---

## What is Microsoft MVP?

Even though it says MVP, it is not about the most valuable player in sports (I get asked this occasionally...).
**Microsoft MVP (Most Valuable Professional)** is an award given to individuals who actively share and disseminate their knowledge and experience regarding Microsoft technologies to the community.

Writing technical blogs, answering questions on Q&A sites,
publishing sample codes, or speaking at events.
"Sharing what you know in a way that helps someone else" -- such steady activities are evaluated.

There are awardees all over the world, but the number is limited every year, and it is not easy to be re-awarded.
Under such circumstances, I am truly grateful to have been recognized again.

---

## Reasons for the Award

This time, I was re-awarded in the "Developer Technologies" category.
Over the past year, I have been doing activities like this:

* Writing blog posts about Microsoft-related technologies
* Posting answers on technical Q&A sites
* Publishing sample codes on GitHub
* Speaking at study groups and technical events, etc.

None of these are extraordinary, but
I have been conscious of disseminating information by turning "things I tried that worked" or "things I stumbled on but managed to solve" into a form that is even slightly useful.

I am genuinely happy that it has taken shape in this way.

---

## Looking Ahead

The world of technology moves incredibly fast, and just keeping up with it is tough.
But precisely because of that, I feel there is meaning in sharing "raw information" like what it was actually like to use it and what was hard to understand.

I hope to continue doing this at my own pace without straining myself.
I would be happy if I could deliver information that makes someone think, "Oh, that helped," in some form or another.

I look forward to your continued support.
