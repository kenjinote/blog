## 1. From Tabulating Machines to IBM
Founded in 1911. It started with punch card tabulating machines used for national censuses and later renamed itself IBM (International Business Machines).

## 2. System/360 and the Mainframe
Announced in 1964, the "System/360" was a historic mainframe (large computer) that provided software compatibility and built the infrastructure for financial and government institutions around the world.

## 3. The IBM PC and Open Architecture
The IBM PC, released in 1981, adopted an open architecture, creating a huge market for "PC/AT compatibles" and spurring the rise of Microsoft and Intel.

## 4. Quantum Computers and Hybrid Cloud
Having sold off its PC and server businesses, IBM continues to evolve today as a company leading the world in the development of "hybrid cloud," "AI," and superconducting quantum computers (IBM Quantum).
