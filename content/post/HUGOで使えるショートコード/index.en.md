# Available Shortcodes in HUGO

## YouTube
`{{</*youtube 123456abc*/>}}`

## Twitter
`{{</*twitter 123456abc*/>}}`

## Instagram
`{{</*instagram 123456abc*/>}}`

## Flickr
`{{</*flickr 123456abc*/>}}`

## Vimeo
`{{</*vimeo 123456abc*/>}}`

## Others
[Shortcodes](https://gohugo.io/content-management/shortcodes/)

## Side note
If you want to display `{{</* foo */>}}` on the site, you need to write `{{</*/* foo */*/>}}`.
