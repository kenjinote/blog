# Using HTML5 UP website templates

Starting from a polished template can help you explore a site's layout and visual design sooner than building every page from scratch. HTML5 UP publishes responsive site templates built with HTML5 and CSS3. Its catalog and availability can change, so check the [official site](https://html5up.net/) for the current listings.

## Choose a template

Open the live demo for designs you like and check the navigation, page structure, and appearance on a phone. Also consider whether the pages and features you need can fit into the template without extensive rework.

## Download and customize

Use the site's “Free Download” link, inspect the HTML, CSS, and image files, then replace the demo content with your own. Treat the download as a starting point: remove demo copy and links that do not belong, and review every page at the screen sizes your visitors use.
