Here is how to create a shortcut to lock your computer.

It is convenient because you can lock your computer by simply double-clicking the shortcut created on your desktop.

## How to Create

#### 1. Right-click on the desktop and select `New` > `Shortcut`

![img_2.png](img_2.png)
#### 2. The Create Shortcut screen will appear. Enter `rundll32.exe user32.dll,LockWorkStation` and click `Next`

![img_1.png](img_1.png)

#### 3. Enter the shortcut name `Lock Computer` and click `Finish`

![img_3.png](img_3.png)

#### 4. Change the icon of the created shortcut

Right-click the created shortcut and select `Properties`

![img_4.png](img_4.png)

Click `Change Icon`

![img_6.png](img_6.png)

Select the yellow padlock icon and click `OK`. Click `OK` again to close the properties.

![img_5.png](img_5.png)

That's it.

![img_7.png](img_7.png)

Double-clicking the created shortcut will lock your computer.

You can also lock your computer by pressing the `Win key` + `L key`.

*Note: No confirmation dialog will be displayed when locking the computer.
