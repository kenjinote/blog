## I thought about why I like mathematics and computers

### Equal to everyone
Regardless of the person, position, or location, it always returns the same output for the same input.
### Clear rules
The rules are clearly defined, and there is no absurdity or injustice.
### The answers and theories derived are forever correct
There is no worry that an answer once derived will be rewritten later.
### Appropriate level of difficulty
There are always unsolved problems, and you can challenge yourself to solve them at any time.
