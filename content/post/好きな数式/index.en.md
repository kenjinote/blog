## Wilson's Theorem
When $p$ is a prime number, $(p−1)!≡−1 \pmod p$ holds.

## Euler's Formula
$e^{iπ}+1=0$

## Fermat's Last Theorem
For any integer $n≥3$, there are no positive integer solutions $x, y, z$ to the equation $x^n+y^n=z^n$.

## Basel Problem
$1/1^2+1/2^2+1/3^2+1/4^2+...=π^2/6$

## Euler's Prime-Generating Polynomial
$n^2+n+41$ is a prime number for all integers $n$ from $0$ to $39$.
