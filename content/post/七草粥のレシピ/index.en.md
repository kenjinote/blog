# Seven Herbs (Nanakusa)
- Seri (Water dropwort)
- Nazuna (Shepherd's purse)
- Gogyo (Cudweed)
- Hakobera (Chickweed)
- Hotokenoza (Nipplewort)
- Suzuna (Turnip)
- Suzushiro (Radish)
