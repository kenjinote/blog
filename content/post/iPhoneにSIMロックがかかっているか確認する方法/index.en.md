# How to Check Whether Your iPhone Is Carrier-Locked

Open Settings > General > About and find Carrier Lock. If it says “No SIM restrictions,” the iPhone is not carrier-locked.

The label can vary with the iOS language or version. If “No SIM restrictions” is not shown or the status is unclear, ask the seller or your current carrier. Only the carrier can unlock an iPhone.

An unlocked phone is not guaranteed to work with every network. Also check network-band compatibility, service eligibility, and eSIM support. Carrier Lock is different from a SIM PIN lock.
