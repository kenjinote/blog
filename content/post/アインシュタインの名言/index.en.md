## The World Doesn't Appreciate Your Good Deeds?

`
The world will never appreciate the million good things you do.
But it will criticize the one wrong thing you do...
But do not be discouraged.
"Always rise above all laughter and criticism. Stay strong."

- Albert Einstein
`

This is a quote attributed to Einstein. It sharply points out the harshness of society and the bias in people's evaluations that we feel every day.

---

## Still, Keep Rising

"Always rise above all laughter and criticism. Stay strong."

This following sentence can be said to be Einstein's answer to the harsh reality mentioned above. It teaches us the importance of not yielding to criticism or ridicule, and continuing to move forward believing in ourselves.

---

## Applying Einstein's Teachings in Daily Life

This quote is deeply related to our daily lives. For example, there are times when your efforts at work go unrecognized, and only trivial mistakes stand out. Also, your statements on social media might be taken out of context and criticized.

At such times, try to remember this quote by Einstein. It is important to hold on to your own beliefs rather than being swayed by the evaluations of others.

---

## Summary

This quote by Einstein teaches us the importance of "living believing in yourself without being swayed by the evaluations of others". Let's have the courage to pursue the path we believe in, without yielding to criticism or ridicule.

Finally, Einstein's words once again.

> **"Always rise above all laughter and criticism. Stay strong."**

I hope this quote resonates with your heart and becomes a catalyst for you to take a positive step forward.
