# Bought a Used ThinkPad Laptop

Since my work on a laptop mostly involves connecting to other machines via remote desktop, I didn't need a high-spec PC and a Chromebook would have been fine. However, because I couldn't find one with a good design and high resolution, I bought a ThinkPad.

The specs are below, and using various points, the price came out to around 55,000 yen.

## ThinkPad T470s

- CPU: Intel Core i7 7600U
- RAM: 24GB
- SSD: 512GB
- OS: Windows 10 Pro 64bit
- Screen: 14.0-inch wide (non-glare panel)
- Resolution: WQHD LCD 2560×1440
- Weight: Approx. 1.32kg
- Notes: English keyboard

Since it's used, I thought it might have signs of wear or scratches, but there were no problems at all, which is great.
The battery is also working fine so far.

## Complaints that come to mind so far
- There are no `Pause` and `App` keys. I used the `Pause` key as a shortcut to open system settings. Also, I used the `App` key to open pop-up menus, so it's inconvenient without it.
- The `PageUp` and `PageDown` keys are right next to the arrow keys on the keyboard, so I often press them by mistake. This might just take some getting used to.
