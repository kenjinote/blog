## About Unsplash

Images covered by the standard Unsplash License can be downloaded for free and used for commercial or non-commercial purposes. You do not have to ask Unsplash or the photographer for permission or provide credit, though credit is appreciated. Do not assume every image has the same license: check labels such as Unsplash+ and the terms for the specific image.

### What to check before using an image

The license does not allow selling an image on its own without significant creative changes or compiling Unsplash images to create a similar or competing service. The Terms also contain additional restrictions, including use in AI training datasets and technologies intended to identify natural persons.

The photographer’s copyright license does not automatically clear every right in what a photo depicts, such as a person’s likeness or privacy, a trademark, artwork, or a venue. Unsplash says some uses may require permission from third parties and places that check on the user. Before using an image in advertising, products, or sensitive contexts, check the rights and any required releases for that image. Unsplash+ has a separate paid license.

Before using a photo, read the license that applies to that image and Unsplash’s current Terms, then obtain any permissions required for your intended use. Even when credit is not required under the standard license, you can acknowledge the photographer as a courtesy.
