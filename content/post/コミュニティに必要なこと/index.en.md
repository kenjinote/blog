## Scratch Community Guidelines

The official Scratch Community Guidelines set out six ideas:

1. **Be respectful.** Remember that people of different ages and backgrounds will see what you share.
2. **Protect safety and privacy.** Do not publish personal details such as real names, phone numbers, or addresses.
3. **Give constructive feedback.** Point out something positive and offer useful suggestions.
4. **Share and remix responsibly.** When building on a Scratch project, credit the creators whose work you use.
5. **Be honest.** Do not impersonate others or spread rumors.
6. **Help keep the space friendly.** Use the report feature for inappropriate content instead of escalating a fight.

These guidelines are designed for Scratch and its audience. Other communities should adapt their rules to their participants, purpose, privacy needs, moderation, and ways to ask for help or report concerns. Scratch is a useful starting point, not a complete operating policy for every group.
