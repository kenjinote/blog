# What a Community Needs

I felt that the Scratch Community Guidelines are very refined and excellent.
The following points were written:

> Treat everyone with respect.

> Keep safe: Do not publish personal information or contact details.

> Leave helpful comments.

> Understand remix culture.

> Be honest.

> Make the site a comfortable place.

I think these perspectives are applicable to any community.
